How the loop works

Every submission travels the same path — nothing disappears into a suggestion box.

1
Submit
Any team member submits an idea — no login needed.
2
Review
Leadership acknowledges every submission and decides what to advance.
3
Plan
An owner, target date, and action steps turn the idea into a real plan.
4
Implement
The improvement is piloted, rolled out, and the outcome recorded.
