# Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval. Core collaboration
hours are 10am–3pm in the employee's local time zone. Equipment (laptop, monitor, headset) is
provided by the company for remote setups; requests go through the IT Portal.

Employees working remotely from outside their home country for more than 2 consecutive weeks
must notify HR in advance for tax and compliance reasons.
