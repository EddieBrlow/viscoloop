Business expenses under $100 can be submitted directly through the Expense Portal with a receipt
attached; no pre-approval needed. Expenses over $100, or any travel booking, require manager
approval before the expense is incurred. Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days of
approval. Meals while traveling for business are capped at $50/day.
